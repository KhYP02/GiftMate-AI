# GiftMate AI — Confidence-Driven Gifting Assistant

GiftMate AI is an AI-powered gifting assistant designed to solve a major but overlooked problem in gifting: decision confidence.

Most gifting platforms focus on providing more options. Through user research and behavioral analysis, we identified that users do not lack options — they lack confidence that their choice is the “right” one.

## Live Prototype

Try the working prototype: [giftmateai.lovable.app](https://giftmateai.lovable.app/)

The prototype is built with Lovable to test the core idea: the app listens, builds a portrait of who you are buying for, and picks gifts that show you were paying attention. It is a prototype for validating the concept, not a production app.

## Problem Statement

Users often:

* Overthink gifting decisions
* Fear choosing inappropriate gifts
* Depend on friends or social validation
* Feel overwhelmed by too many generic suggestions

## Research & Insights

* Conducted 15+ user interviews
* Analyzed 80+ survey responses
* Found that 41.5% users rely on guesswork while choosing gifts
* Less than 20% directly validate with recipients

## Core Features

### Visual Gift Matching

Users upload a reference image and AI identifies style patterns to suggest similar gift ideas.

### Persona-Based AI Companion

Users interact with different AI personas for more personalized and relatable recommendations.

### Multi-Language AI Interaction

Supports regional language conversations for improved accessibility and comfort.

## Frameworks Used

* RICE Prioritization
* GTM Planning
* User Persona Mapping
* Behavioral Analysis
* North Star Metrics

## Key Metrics

* % Users Confident in Their Gift Choice
* Recommendation Acceptance Rate
* Time-to-Decision
* Repeat Usage Rate

## Outcome

The project reframed gifting from a “search problem” to a “confidence problem,” helping build a more thoughtful and emotionally aware gifting experience.
